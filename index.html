John Nicholson

I am currently a Postdoctoral Research Fellow at the Max Planck Institute for Mathematics in Bonn. Previously, I was a Heilbronn Research Fellow at Imperial College London and a Rankin-Sneddon Research Fellow at the University of Glasgow. In October 2021, I completed my PhD at University College London under the supervision of F. E. A. Johnson. Before that, I was a student at the University of Cambridge.

Research Interests

My research is on classification problems in low-dimensional topology and their interactions with areas of algebra such as homological group theory and integral representation theory. My time is currently split between the following closely-related topics:

  • The homotopy/homeomorphism classification of 4-dimensional manifolds, as well as in higher dimensions
  • The topology of group presentations and 2-dimensional cell complexes (see Wall's 1979 Problems List)
  • Integral representation theory, particularly the study of projective modules over integral group rings
