About

Mathematics, computation and problem solving.

My academic research is in low-dimensional topology, group theory and integral representation theory. I was a Rankin–Sneddon Research Fellow at the University of Glasgow and, before that, a Heilbronn Research Fellow at Imperial College London. I completed my PhD at University College London under the supervision of F. E. A. Johnson, after studying mathematics at the University of Cambridge.

In 2026 I joined the Max Planck Institute for Mathematics in Bonn as a postdoctoral researcher. I also hold an honorary research affiliation with the University of Glasgow.

Research

Research interests

01

4-manifolds

Homotopy, homeomorphism and stable classification problems for 4-dimensional manifolds, with related questions in higher dimensions.

02

2-complexes

The topology of group presentations and finite 2-dimensional cell complexes, including problems originating in Wall's finiteness and D2 theory.

03

Integral group rings

Projective and stably free modules over integral group rings, cancellation phenomena, and their applications to topology.
