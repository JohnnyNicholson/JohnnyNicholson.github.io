Algebraic Methods in 4-Manifold Topology

19th - 23rd June at the University of Glasgow

The aim of this workshop is to bring together a small group of experts on the application of algebraic methods to the classification of non-simply connected 4-manifolds. This meeting is supported by a focused research grant from the Heilbronn Institute for Mathematical Research.
